Briefcloud
How it works

From spoken bug to filed ticket, in one take.

1

Record

Hit the mark on any tab and talk through the bug while it is on screen. Stop when you are done. Brief captures the tab, your voice, screenshots, and the console errors.

2

It writes it

Brief transcribes what you said, reads the screenshots and runtime errors, and drafts a clear, structured ticket with reproduction steps and evidence.

3

Filed

The ticket is created in your Linear or Jira, with the right project, status, and attachments. No copy and paste, no local agent to run.

Connect your tracker once on the web, pair the extension, and every brief you record files itself. Linear & Jira supported.
